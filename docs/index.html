NFL Analytics Simulator

Research-grounded, NFL-only analytics and simulation. Reconstruct real games play by play, see how expected points and win probability move, and understand fourth-down calls in plain football language.

Local-first and CPU-only. The public demo runs on example data with no live feed. No sports-betting features.

Try it

Fan demo →Branded home, Game Explorer and the 4th-&-short explainer. Game Explorer →Walk through real games play by play. 4th-down decision →Go, punt or kick: what the numbers say and why. Fourth-down quiz →Make the call yourself, then compare with the model.
